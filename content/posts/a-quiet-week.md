Last week I cleared the calendar, borrowed a small field recorder and went outside with no plan except to listen.

## Monday to Wednesday

The first two days were restless. I kept reaching for my phone to check things that did not need checking. By Wednesday the urge faded, and I started noticing sounds I normally filter out: the hum of a refrigerator, a neighbor's wind chimes, rain on a metal roof.

## Thursday to Sunday

I recorded a little every day. Nothing dramatic. A market at seven in the morning. Footsteps on gravel. A kettle coming to the boil.

What surprised me was how much a plain recording says about a place. No narration, no music, and still you know exactly where you are.

### What I would do again

- Walk the same route at different times of day.
- Record for ten minutes, then stop and just listen.
- Write down one sentence about each recording before moving on.

Some of these recordings will turn up in the podcast. The first episode explains why I started in the first place.
